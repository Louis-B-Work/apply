# Apply form redesign prototype

An interactive prototype of three redesigned steps of the Car Finance 247 apply form: address, date of birth and contact details.

Open the site and use the bar at the bottom to switch step. The card beside each page lists the hypotheses being tested; use its switches to turn individual changes on or off, and the bottom-bar switch for the four-stage progress stepper.

This is a design prototype with sample data. It does not collect or send anything.
