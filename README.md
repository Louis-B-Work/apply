# Apply form redesign prototype

An interactive prototype of three redesigned steps of the Car Finance 247 apply form: address, date of birth and contact details.

Open the site and use the bar at the bottom to switch step. The Changes to test card beside each page lists the changes being tested; use its switches to turn individual changes on or off, or Show in the bar to jump between the live site and all changes. Choose Compare in the bar to see your changes next to the live site, and Share to copy a link to the same setup.

This is a design prototype with sample data. It does not collect or send anything.
